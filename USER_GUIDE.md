## Introduction

The A-Player Hiring Suite helps you make better hiring and leadership decisions by implementing proven methodologies from bestselling books on talent assessment.

### What Makes This Different?

- **Evidence-Based**: Built on proven frameworks from "Who," "Topgrading," "The CEO Next Door," and more
- **Local-First**: All your data stays on your computer—no cloud, no subscriptions
- **Structured**: Replace gut feelings with repeatable assessment processes
- **Comprehensive**: Covers hiring, leadership evaluation, and development planning

## Creating Scorecards

### What is an A-Method Scorecard?

Based on "Who: The A Method for Hiring," a scorecard defines what success looks like in a role BEFORE you start interviewing. It includes:

1. **Mission**: One-sentence elevator pitch for the role
2. **Outcomes**: 3-8 measurable results expected
3. **Competencies**: 5-8 skills/behaviors required

### Creating Your First Scorecard

1. Click **"Scorecards"** in the sidebar
2. Click **"Create Scorecard"**
3. **Define the Mission:**
   - Example: "Lead product engineering to ship high-quality features that delight customers"
   - Keep it to 1-2 sentences
   - Focus on *why* the role exists

4. **Add Outcomes (3-8 recommended):**
   - Make them SMART (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound)
   - Examples:
     - "Launch mobile app by Q3 with 4.5+ star rating"
     - "Reduce customer churn from 8% to 5% within 12 months"
     - "Hire and onboard 10 engineers within 6 months"

5. **Define Competencies (5-8 recommended):**
   - List the skills and behaviors needed
   - For each competency, add behavioral indicators
   - Examples:
     - **Strategic Thinking**
       - Identifies patterns across complex information
       - Anticipates market shifts and adjusts plans
       - Balances short-term execution with long-term vision
     - **People Development**
       - Provides specific, actionable feedback
       - Creates growth opportunities for team members
       - Addresses performance issues promptly

6. Click **"Save Scorecard"**
7. Click **"Export PDF"** to share with your hiring team

### Scorecard Best Practices

- Create scorecards BEFORE posting jobs
- Get input from the hiring team
- Revisit and refine based on hiring outcomes
- Use scorecards consistently across all candidates for the role
- Store template scorecards for common roles

## Conducting Interviews

### Biographical Interview Overview

Based on Topgrading methodology, biographical interviews:
- Walk chronologically through a candidate's career
- Ask consistent questions about each job
- Use TORC (Threat of Reference Check) to encourage honesty
- Identify patterns across multiple roles

### Setting Up an Interview

1. Click **"Interviews"** in the sidebar
2. Click **"Schedule Interview"**
3. Select the candidate
4. Link to relevant scorecard (optional but recommended)
5. Add the candidate's **work history**:
   - Company name
   - Job title
   - Start and end dates
   - Key responsibilities

6. Click **"Generate Interview Script"**

### The Interview Script

The system generates a comprehensive script including:

**For Each Job:**
1. **Job Overview**
   - What were your main responsibilities?
   - Who did you report to?
   - Who reported to you?

2. **Accomplishments**
   - What were your biggest achievements?
   - What specific results did you deliver?
   - What are you most proud of?

3. **Mistakes & Learnings**
   - What challenges did you face?
   - What would you do differently?
   - What did you learn?

4. **Boss Relationship**
   - Tell me about your relationship with your boss
   - What would they say were your strengths?
   - What would they say you needed to improve?
   - How would they rate your performance 1-10?

5. **TORC (Threat of Reference Check)**
   - "I'll be calling your boss for a reference. What will they tell me?"
   - This encourages candidates to be honest upfront

6. **Reason for Leaving**
   - Why did you leave this role?
   - How did you find your next opportunity?

### During the Interview

1. **Take Notes** directly in the application
2. **Rate Responses** on a 1-5 scale
3. **Flag Red Flags** immediately:
   - Inconsistencies in timeline
   - Evasive answers
   - Negative patterns (always blaming others)
   - Reference concerns

### After the Interview

1. Complete your notes and ratings
2. Add overall assessment:
   - Overall Rating (A, B, or C player)
   - Hire recommendation (Yes/No)
   - Summary notes
3. Export to PDF for hiring team review

### Interview Best Practices

- **Time**: Block 2-3 hours for thorough chronological interview
- **Preparation**: Review resume and scorecard beforehand
- **Approach**: Be warm but probe for specifics
- **Red Flags**: Trust your instincts—patterns matter
- **References**: ALWAYS check references with former bosses

## CEO Behaviors Assessment

### Overview

Based on "The CEO Next Door" by Botelho & Powell, which studied 17,000 executive assessments to identify four behaviors that distinguish successful CEOs.

### The Four Behaviors

1. **Decide with Speed and Conviction**
   - Makes tough calls quickly
   - Commits to decisions even with incomplete information
   - Communicates decisions clearly

2. **Engage for Impact**
   - Builds strong stakeholder relationships
   - Influences without authority
   - Creates powerful coalitions

3. **Relentless Reliability**
   - Consistently delivers on commitments
   - Known as someone who always follows through
   - Sets realistic expectations and meets them

4. **Adapt Boldly**
   - Navigates change and ambiguity well
   - Sees opportunities in challenges
   - Willing to pivot when needed

### Conducting a CEO Behaviors Assessment

1. Click **"CEO Assessments"**
2. Click **"New Assessment"**
3. Select assessment type:
   - **Self-Assessment**: The person rates themselves
   - **Observer**: You rate someone else
   - **360**: Multiple people rate the same person

4. For each behavior:
   - Rate 1-5 (1 = Needs Development, 5 = Exceptional)
   - Provide specific evidence examples
   - Add development notes if relevant

5. Save the assessment

### Interpreting Results

**Score Ranges:**
- **4.5-5.0**: Exceptional strength
- **4.0-4.4**: Above average
- **3.0-3.9**: Average/Adequate
- **2.0-2.9**: Below average—development needed
- **1.0-1.9**: Critical gap—major focus area

**Look for:**
- **Strengths**: Which behaviors score 4.0+?
- **Gaps**: Which behaviors score below 3.0?
- **Patterns**: Do multiple assessors agree?
- **Extremes**: Are there outlier behaviors?

### Using Results

- **Hiring**: Look for strengths aligned with role needs
- **Development**: Focus on 1-2 gaps, not everything
- **Promotion**: Ensure behaviors match next level
- **Coaching**: Use as basis for development plan

## Power Score Assessment

### Overview

Power Score assesses leadership effectiveness across three dimensions:
- **Results**: Business outcomes delivered
- **Relationships**: Trust and influence with others
- **Role Model**: Values and integrity

### Conducting a Power Score Assessment

1. Click **"Power Score"**
2. Click **"New Assessment"**
3. Select the subject (candidate)
4. Answer questions for each dimension on 1-5 scale
5. The system calculates dimension scores and overall Power Score

### Understanding Your Power Score

**Scoring:**
- Responses are on a 1-5 scale
- Converted to 0-100 scale per dimension
- Overall Power Score is the average

**Rating Labels:**
- **85-100**: Exceptional
- **70-84**: Strong
- **50-69**: Adequate
- **30-49**: Needs Development
- **0-29**: Critical Gap

### Results Dimension

**High Results Score (85+):**
- Consistently delivers business outcomes
- Exceeds targets and goals
- Known for "getting things done"

**Low Results Score (<50):**
- Struggles to deliver outcomes
- Misses targets frequently
- Needs more focus on execution

### Relationships Dimension

**High Relationships Score (85+):**
- High trust with team and stakeholders
- Strong network and influence
- People want to work with them

**Low Relationships Score (<50):**
- Burned bridges or friction
- Limited influence or network
- Trust issues with others

### Role Model Dimension

**High Role Model Score (85+):**
- Strong integrity and values
- Others look to them as example
- "Walks the talk"

**Low Role Model Score (<50):**
- Ethical concerns
- Misalignment of words and actions
- Values questions

### Creating Development Plans

After assessment:
1. Identify the lowest dimension
2. Click **"Add Development Plan"**
3. Set a specific goal
4. Define action steps
5. Set target date
6. Track progress over time

### Power Score Best Practices

- Assess leaders every 6-12 months to track trends
- Use trends view to see improvement over time
- Focus development on lowest dimension first
- Balance is key—all three dimensions matter
- Export to PDF for performance reviews

## Data Management

### Backing Up Your Data

**Important**: Your data lives ONLY on your computer. Back it up regularly!

1. Navigate to **"Settings"**
2. Under "Data Backup & Export":
   - **SQLite Export**: Full database backup (recommended)
   - **JSON Export**: Portable format for sharing

3. **Backup Schedule Recommendation:**
   - After each important assessment
   - Weekly if actively hiring
   - Monthly minimum

4. **Storage Locations:**
   - Local external drive
   - Network drive
   - Personal cloud (Dropbox, Google Drive, etc.)

### Restoring from Backup

1. Navigate to **"Settings"**
2. Click **"Import Backup"**
3. Select your `.db` or `.json` file
4. Confirm (this will overwrite existing data)
5. Refresh your browser

⚠️ **Always backup current data before importing!**

### Exporting Reports

**Scorecards:**
- Navigate to scorecard
- Click "Export PDF"
- Share with hiring team

**Interviews:**
- Navigate to interview
- Click "Export PDF"
- Use for team debrief or reference checks

**Assessments:**
- Navigate to assessment
- Click "Export PDF"
- Use for performance reviews or development discussions

## Best Practices

### Hiring Process

1. **Define the Role** (Create Scorecard First)
2. **Source Candidates** (Add to system)
3. **Screen** (Phone screens, reference checks)
4. **Interview** (Biographical interview)
5. **Assess** (Score against scorecard)
6. **Check References** (Use TORC insights)
7. **Decide** (Hire only A-players)

### Leadership Assessment

1. **Baseline** (Initial CEO Behaviors & Power Score)
2. **360 Feedback** (Multiple observers)
3. **Development Plan** (Focus on gaps)
4. **Regular Check-ins** (Monthly progress)
5. **Reassess** (Every 6-12 months)
6. **Track Trends** (Improvement over time)

### Data Quality

- **Be Consistent**: Use the same process for all candidates in a role
- **Be Specific**: Vague notes = useless later
- **Be Timely**: Enter data immediately after interviews
- **Be Thorough**: Complete all sections—they matter later

### Privacy & Ethics

- **Consent**: Inform candidates they're being assessed
- **Confidentiality**: Keep assessment data secure
- **Fairness**: Use same criteria for all candidates
- **Legal**: Avoid questions about protected classes
- **References**: Get permission before contacting

## FAQ

### General

**Q: Does this application require internet?**
A: No! It runs entirely on your local machine. No cloud, no subscriptions.

**Q: Where is my data stored?**
A: In a SQLite database at `backend/data/hiring_suite.db` on your computer.

**Q: Can multiple people use this?**
A: It's designed for single-user use. For teams, each person runs their own instance.

**Q: Can I customize the methodologies?**
A: The core methodologies are built-in, but you can adapt scorecards and questions to your context.

### Technical

**Q: What if I can't access localhost:8000?**
A: Check that the backend is running and port 8000 isn't blocked by firewall.

**Q: How do I update the application?**
A: Pull latest code from repository, reinstall dependencies, rebuild frontend.

**Q: Can I run this on a server?**
A: Yes, but it's designed for local use. Be mindful of data security if hosting.

### Methodology

**Q: How long should a chronological interview take?**
A: 2-3 hours for thorough coverage of career history. Don't rush.

**Q: What's an "A Player"?**
A: Someone who ranks in the top 10% of talent available for the role at the compensation level offered.

**Q: Should I use all modules for every candidate?**
A: No. Use Scorecards + Interviews for hiring. Use CEO/Power Score for leadership assessment.

**Q: How many candidates should I interview per role?**
A: Interview 3-5 A-player candidates to have options. Don't hire B-players.

### Data

**Q: Can I export data to Excel?**
A: Export to JSON, then import to Excel. Or use the API for custom exports.

**Q: What happens if I delete a candidate?**
A: All associated data (interviews, assessments) is also deleted. Use carefully.

**Q: Can I merge duplicate candidates?**
A: Not currently. Avoid duplicates by checking before adding.

## Appendix: Scoring Guidelines

### Scorecard Ratings

- **A Player**: Top 10% of talent available for the role
- **B Player**: Top 50% but not top 10%
- **C Player**: Bottom 50%—avoid hiring

### Interview Question Ratings

**5 - Exceptional:**
- Specific, quantified examples
- Demonstrates exceptional competency
- Would rank in top 10%

**4 - Strong:**
- Good examples with some quantification
- Demonstrates strong competency
- Would rank in top 25%

**3 - Adequate:**
- Acceptable response
- Meets minimum bar
- Middle 50%

**2 - Below Average:**
- Vague or weak examples
- Some concerns
- Bottom 25%

**1 - Poor:**
- No examples or evasive
- Red flags present
- Would not hire
